# Intonation

A website for exploring the math and acoustics of tuning, chords and sound waves, aimed first at curious musicians, with deeper material available for specialists.

## Tuning

**Tuning system**:
Any rule that assigns a pitch to each note.
_Avoid_: Tuning (on its own), scale, temperament (when the system isn't tempered)

**Temperament**:
A tuning system that deliberately makes some pure intervals slightly impure so that others work better, e.g. equal temperament and meantone.
_Avoid_: Using it as a synonym for tuning system

**Intonation**:
How in tune a specific chord or performance actually is, measured against pure ratios.
_Avoid_: Tuning system, temperament

**Equal division of the octave (EDO)**:
A temperament that splits the octave into N identical steps, written N-EDO; 12-EDO is standard equal temperament.
_Avoid_: TET (except in "12-TET" as a familiar label), ET

**Step**:
One of the N equal divisions of the octave in an EDO, numbered from 0; EDO notes are named by step (e.g. "step 10 of 31").
_Avoid_: Key, degree, microtone

**EDO mapping**:
The step an EDO assigns to each prime (its best fifth for 3, best major third for 5, best seventh for 7), from which every pure interval in that EDO is built.
_Avoid_: Val, patent val (specialist jargon), nearest step (a different, note-by-note rule)

**Cent**:
The unit of interval size on a logarithmic scale: 1200 cents to the octave, 100 to a 12-EDO semitone; written ¢.
_Avoid_: Semitone percentage, savart

**Tonic**:
The note a tuning system is built from (e.g. meantone "in C"); other than in an EDO, changing it changes which keys sound in tune.
_Avoid_: Root (that belongs to a chord), key, home note

**Pure interval**:
An interval whose frequencies form an exact small whole-number ratio, such as 3/2 or 5/4.
_Avoid_: Perfect (that's an interval name), just (that names a tuning system), natural

**Wolf**:
The one badly out-of-tune fifth left over where a tuning system's chain of fifths closes (e.g. G♯–E♭ in meantone in C).
_Avoid_: Wolf tone (a different, instrument-body phenomenon), bad fifth

**Comma**:
A tiny interval left over when two routes to "the same" note disagree, e.g. the syntonic comma 81/80 (21.5¢) between four pure fifths and a pure major third plus two octaves.
_Avoid_: Error, drift (drift is what a comma causes)

**Comma pump**:
A chord progression of pure chords, each holding a note shared with the previous one, that returns to its starting chord shifted by a comma.
_Avoid_: Comma drift (the effect, not the progression), pitch drift

**Spelling**:
The historical name a 12-note tuning system gives each of its keys (e.g. meantone has a G♯ but no A♭); a chord needing the other name borrows that key.
_Avoid_: Enharmonic (as a noun), label

**Limit**:
The largest prime allowed in a pure interval's ratio: 5-limit uses only 2, 3 and 5 (e.g. 5/4, 6/5); 7-limit also allows 7 (e.g. 7/4).
_Avoid_: Prime limit (fine in prose, but use "N-limit" as the label), odd limit (a different measure)

**Reference pitch**:
The frequency assigned to A4 (440 Hz by default), from which every other pitch is calculated.
_Avoid_: Concert pitch, base frequency, A440 (when another value is set)

## Sound

**Timbre**:
The tone colour of a note, defined by the strengths of its harmonics.
_Avoid_: Instrument, voice, patch

**Harmonic**:
One of the sine components of a note, at a whole-number multiple (1×, 2×, 3× …) of its fundamental frequency.
_Avoid_: Overtone (off by one: the first overtone is the 2nd harmonic), partial (fine for chords pooling several notes' harmonics)

**Timbre preset**:
One of the site's ready-made Timbres (sine, organ-like, string-like, brass-like, voice-like).
_Avoid_: Sound, instrument

**Beats**:
The slow, regular wobble in loudness heard when two partials are a few hertz apart; the beat rate equals their frequency difference.
_Avoid_: Wobble (fine in prose), vibrato, tremolo

**Roughness**:
The harsh, buzzing quality heard when partials are close but too far apart to beat slowly; one ingredient of how consonant a chord sounds, not the whole of it.
_Avoid_: Dissonance, consonance (these include more than roughness), harshness

**Custom Timbre**:
The single user-built Timbre, made by editing a Timbre preset's harmonics in the Sound waves Lab and usable in every Lab.
_Avoid_: User timbre, saved sound

## Site

**Lab**:
A self-contained interactive tool on the site, focused on one topic (e.g. Chord comparison).
_Avoid_: Page, module, chapter, feature

**The math**:
The part of every Lab that explains, in formulas and worked numbers, exactly what is on screen right now.
_Avoid_: Theory, explanation, details, maths panel

## Chords

**Chord comparison**:
Playing the same chord in several tuning systems side by side to hear and see how they differ.
_Avoid_: Chord tuning

**Root**:
The note a chord is named after and built on.
_Avoid_: Tonic, bass (the bass is the lowest note, which differs in inversions)

**Pure chord**:
A chord whose notes are tuned in 5-limit pure intervals above its root; the target that every tuning system's version is measured against.
_Avoid_: Just chord, ideal chord, perfect chord

**Alternative pure chord**:
A named non-default pure chord offered where theory genuinely disagrees, such as the harmonic 7th (4:5:6:7) for a dominant 7th.
_Avoid_: Variant, alternate tuning
